StedetThe place

Huser Gård

En gammel gård i Fenstad med jorder, gamle trær og vannet rett nedenfor. Vielsen holdes ute ved bryggen, festen inne på gården.

An old farm in Fenstad with meadows, ancient trees and the water just below. The ceremony is held outside by the jetty, the celebration inside the farm.
